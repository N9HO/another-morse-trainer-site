Built for real copy

Everything here exists to make you a faster, more confident operator — not to pass a quiz.

The Koch method

Start at full speed with two characters and add one at a time. You learn rhythm and sound, never dots-and-dashes on paper.

Time-to-recognize tracking

Every answer is timed from the last tone. The app knows not just whether you got it — but how fast — and drills what's still slow.

A leveled Journey

A gamified path from letters through prosigns, Q-codes and call signs — with an unlock map, saved progress, and a bar that drains when you miss.

Confusion drills

It quietly learns the pairs you actually mix up (B/D, U/V, S/H…) and builds targeted sessions to sort them out.

Hands-free Listen & Learn

Passive practice for the car or a walk: code plays, then the answer is spoken aloud — with full background audio and lock-screen controls.

Send, don't just copy

Key it back on a touch key, a hardware MIDI key, or a Vail Adapter — with live decode. Or print drill sheets built from what you've studied.

Decode off the air

Point the microphone at your rig's speaker or a WebSDR and read received Morse as text, with live WPM and pitch telemetry.

Work a real pileup

Call CQ against a simulated pileup with QSB, QRN, cut numbers and busted calls — or run a timed leg of a real weekly contest.

Progress you can see

Daily streak, per-character accuracy, median recognition time, most-confused pairs, and performance by speed band — weakest first.

One character at a time

A staged ladder takes you from single letters to pairs, words, and call signs. Each character graduates only when you can copy it reliably and quickly.

  • Sound-alike answer choices that mirror real on-air confusion
  • Auto-advance on correct, instant reveal on a miss
  • Answer by tapping, typing, speaking, or keying it back
  • Opt-in punctuation and prosigns when you're ready

An honest trainer, built by a ham

Another Morse Trainer is a free, open-source app for iPhone and Android. It was built the way a CW operator actually learns — by ear, at speed, focusing relentlessly on what you get wrong. No accounts, no ads, no tracking: everything stays on your device.
