Support the project

Another Morse Trainer is free and open source, with no ads, no subscriptions, and no tracking. That is not going to change. It is built and maintained by one ham, N9HO, in the hours around a day job.

Buy me a coffee

If the app has helped your copy and you want to say thanks, a coffee goes a long way. It covers the Apple and Google developer fees, the domain, and the CI minutes that build and test every release, and it is a real morale boost on the nights spent chasing a timing bug nobody will ever notice.

☕ Buy me a coffee

One-off or monthly, whatever suits. Nothing in the app is locked behind it, and it never will be.

Other ways to help

  • Tell another operator. Word of mouth in a club or on the air is how most people find the app.
  • Join the Discord. Bug reports, feature chat, and the people who hear new builds first.
  • Test a beta. iOS builds go out on TestFlight; ask in Discord for Android closed testing.
  • Leave a review on the App Store or Google Play. Reviews are what the store algorithms actually read.
  • Contribute on GitHub. Issues, fixtures, and pull requests are all welcome. The README explains how the two ports are kept in step.
