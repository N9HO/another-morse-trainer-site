Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 13, 2026

Another Morse Trainer (“the app”) is a Morse code learning app. The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, and has no accounts, ads, or analytics.

Information the app stores

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app’s private storage:

  • Your settings (code speed, Farnsworth spacing, sidetone pitch, haptics, reminder time, voice-answer preference).
  • Your practice progress (daily streak, session history, and per-character recognition times).

This information never leaves your device, is not sent to the developer or any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Microphone

The app requests microphone access only for the optional “Voice answers” feature, which lets you speak your answer instead of tapping it. When you use this feature, your speech is transcribed using your device’s built-in speech recognition (for example, Apple’s on iOS or Google’s on Android), which may process the audio to convert it to text.

The app itself does not record, save, or transmit any audio. The microphone is used only while you actively start a voice answer, and the feature is off by default.

Notifications

If you enable the daily practice reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. No notification data is sent anywhere.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking SDKs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
